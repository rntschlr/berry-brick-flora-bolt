Description
  Tinta is a Hungarian grammar notebook for English speakers.

  Learn the 40-letter alphabet, vowel harmony, the eighteen noun cases,
  definite vs indefinite conjugation, possession, and the phrases you
  actually need on the street. Interactive labs decline a noun or
  conjugate a verb as you type. A twenty-question drill sits at the end.

  Progress stays on this device: opened sheets, bookmarks, and drill
  scores.
